Privacy Policy

App: Empire Tactics: Conquest (com.tdp.empiretactics)
Developer: TDP Studio
Contact: phuongtd57@gmail.com
Effective date: 24 November 2026

Empire Tactics: Conquest is a single-player strategy game. You can install it and play it all the way through without an account, without signing in, and without giving us your name, your email address or any other personal detail. We do not ask for them and we have no way to collect them.

The game shows no advertising. It contains no analytics and no tracking. Two optional features — cloud save and in-app purchases — involve Google, and those are described below.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress — cleared maps, stars, talents, unlocked units and your diamond balance — is saved in a file on your own device. It never leaves the device unless you choose to back it up.

Settings such as sound volume and your daily gift count are stored in the device's own app preferences. They stay on the device.

What leaves your device, and only if you choose it

Cloud save — Google Play Games Services. Only if you connect it. The game then reads your Play Games player ID and display name, so it can show you which account is connected, and stores a copy of your save file in your own Play Games storage. You can disconnect at any time, and the game shows you both copies side by side before anything is overwritten in either direction.

Purchases — Google Play Billing. If you buy diamonds, Google Play processes the payment. We never see or receive your card details, your billing address or your Google account. To stop a purchase being credited twice, the app creates an anonymous identifier through Firebase Authentication: a random value generated on your device that is not derived from, and cannot be traced back to, you or your Google account. That identifier and the purchase token are sent to our verification service, which asks Google whether the purchase is genuine and records that the diamonds were granted. The service runs on Google Cloud in Singapore (asia-southeast1).

What is not collected

The game contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. It does not collect an advertising ID. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, calendar, phone number or files. It does not track you across other apps or websites. It sends nothing at all unless you connect cloud save or make a purchase.

Children

The game is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through this app, write to phuongtd57@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Cloud save. Optional. Connect or disconnect at any time from the game's Settings screen.
  • Purchases. Optional. The game is fully playable without them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes the local save and all settings from your device.

The cloud copy lives in your own Google Play Games storage; you can delete it there, from the Play Games app, under the game's saved data.

The verification records — an anonymous identifier and the purchase tokens attached to it — can be deleted on request. Write to phuongtd57@gmail.com from the device in question and we will delete them within 30 days. Note that Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases independently of us; that one is governed by Google's policy, not this one.
